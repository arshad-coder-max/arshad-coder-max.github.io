Our Story

Every Thread Carries a Weaver's Legacy

Tantushala was born from a simple belief: that the ancient art of handloom weaving deserves to thrive in the modern world. We work directly with master weavers across India — from the silk workshops of Kanchipuram to the carpet villages of Mirzapur.

By eliminating middlemen, we ensure that artisans receive fair wages and complete creative freedom, while you receive genuinely authentic textiles at transparent prices.

Each product comes with a Weaver Certificate — telling you the name of the artisan who made it, their village, and the tradition behind the craft.
