Our Story

Where Threads
Become Heirlooms

"We don't sell fabric. We carry forward a living inheritance."

TĀNTU was born from a single conviction: that the most extraordinary textiles on earth are vanishing, not because the world doesn't want them — but because it cannot find them.

We travel to over 40 weaving clusters across India, building long-term relationships with artisan families, ensuring fair wages, and bringing their extraordinary work to those who truly value it.
